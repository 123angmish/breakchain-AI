## 🔍 In-Depth Tool Specifications

### 1. 🧠 SoulBot AI Counseling Chamber (`soulbot.html` & `soulbot-widget.js`)
* **Overview:** A conversational AI mental health counselor trained in attachment theory, grief recovery, and cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT).
* **Key Features:**
  - **Speech-to-Text & Text-to-Speech:** Full voice interaction using Web Speech API.
  - **Multilingual Mastery:** Fluent in English, Hindi, and natural Hinglish.
  - **Crisis Safety Net:** Scans for acute distress (self-harm, suicidal ideation) and immediately renders Tele-MANAS (`14416`), KIRAN (`1800-599-0019`), and US (`988`) hotlines.
  - **Multi-Model Provider Switcher (⚙️):** Toggle between Built-in Neural Engine, Google Gemini 2.0 Flash, OpenAI GPT-4o, and Groq Llama 3.3.
  - **Global Widget:** Available on every webpage as a floating interactive bubble.

---

### 2. 🔬 AI Chat Autopsy (`chat.html`)
* **Overview:** Natural Language Processing (NLP) chat analyzer that dissects message history to uncover psychological manipulation.
* **Analysis Metrics:**
  - **Toxicity Score (0–100%):** Overall unhealthy communication density.
  - **Communication Health Score (0–100%):** Balance and reciprocity index.
  - **Empathy Index (0–100%):** Presence of emotional validation and understanding.
* **Flagged Toxic Patterns:**
  - 🚩 *Gaslighting:* Denial of shared reality ("you're crazy", "you're overreacting").
  - 🚩 *Blame-Shifting:* Evasion of accountability ("you made me do this").
  - 🚩 *Emotional Manipulation:* Conditional love and guilt-tripping ("if you loved me").
  - 🚩 *Stonewalling:* Punitive silent treatment ("whatever", "don't talk to me").
  - 🚩 *Emotional Invalidation:* Minimization of legitimate feelings ("stop being dramatic").

### 3. ✉️ AI Closure Letter & Quote Studio (`close.html`)
* **Overview:** Helps individuals achieve psychological closure without needing to reopen communication with their ex-partner.
* **Modes & Tones:**

  2. **Firm Boundary (No-Contact):** Unambiguous boundary setting ending toxic cycles.
  3. **Empowered Rebirth:** Reclaiming self-worth, inner power, and joy.
  4. **Unsent Catharsis:** Raw, unfiltered emotional release for journaling.
  5. **Gratitude & Wisdom:** Honoring life lessons while moving forward.
* **Export Options:** Audio voice playback, 1-click clipboard copy, and text keepsake download.
